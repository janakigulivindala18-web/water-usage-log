BETTER HABITS

Simple ways to save water

Choose a small change you can keep doing.

🚿

Shorten showers

Try a shorter shower and turn off the water while soaping up.

🪥

Close the tap

Turn off the tap while brushing teeth or scrubbing dishes.

🧺

Full loads only

Run the washing machine when you have a full load, when practical.

🌱

Water plants wisely

Water plants in cooler parts of the day to reduce evaporation.

🔧

Fix leaks

Check taps and pipes for drips and arrange repairs promptly.

🍽️

Use a basin

Wash vegetables in a bowl instead of leaving the tap running.
